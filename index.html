A little about me

Hairdressing & beauty, the way it should feel

I'm Anne, a fully qualified mobile hairdresser and beauty therapist based in Draycott, near Derby. Whether you'd prefer to relax at home or pop into my converted-garage studio for a quieter space all of your own, I bring the same warm, friendly, salon-quality service to you.

From everyday cuts and colour to nails, brows and special-occasion styling, every appointment is unhurried, personal, and tailored to you — because beauty should fit around your life, not the other way round.
