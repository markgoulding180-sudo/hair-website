About Anne

A trusted local hairdresser & beauty therapist

Hair, Nails & Beauty by Anne Rusz is a friendly, professional hairdressing and beauty salon based at 6 Victoria Road, Draycott — also offering a mobile hairdresser and home salon service across Long Eaton, Borrowash, Breaston, Sawley and the surrounding areas of Derbyshire.

Anne brings years of experience in hairdressing, facials, massage, waxing, nails and Crown Revival hair systems, with a relaxed, personal approach. Whether you'd prefer to visit the salon or have your appointment at home, you'll receive the same high standard of care.

Open Tuesday to Saturday, with late night appointments on Tuesday and Thursday evenings. Vouchers are available, and a cancellation policy applies to all bookings.
